# Changelog

What's new in each version of StreamKEK, newest first.

## 1.6.0 - 2026-11-13

- **Audio only (Alt+A):** a headphones button in Twitch's player plays a live stream or a Twitch video with sound and no picture, so the picture isn't downloaded.
- **Reload player on errors:** when Twitch's player stops with an error like #2000, it reloads by itself, also in a background tab. It's on by default, under Player.
- **Keep quality in background tabs:** stops Twitch from lowering a live stream to 360p about a minute after you switch to another tab. It's off by default, under Player.
- **See deleted messages:** keep reading a message in Twitch chat after a moderator deletes it, shown faded and crossed out. It's off by default, under Chat.

## 1.5.0 - 2026-11-11

- **Go-live notifications:** get a notification when a channel in your Favorites starts a stream on Twitch, YouTube or Kick, also with no Twitch tab open. It's off by default, under Notifications.
- **Resize streams in multi-stream:** drag between two streams to make one bigger and the other smaller, and double-click to put the sizes back.
- **Sound bars in multi-stream:** moving bars show which stream has the sound.
- **Settings window:** every category is on one page, and the list on the left scrolls to each one.
- **Fixed:** the Stream quality setting now applies to Twitch streams in multi-stream.

## 1.4.2 - 2026-11-10

- The README and the bug report and feature request forms now name Kick alongside Twitch and YouTube.

## 1.4.1 - 2026-11-08

- **Fixed:** the star on a favorite shows again, instead of an empty circle.

## 1.4.0 - 2026-11-07

- **Emotes in the chat box:** a 7TV, BTTV or FFZ emote you type turns into its image.
- **Emote picker:** a button in the chat box opens a searchable list of the channel's and the global emotes.
- **Autocomplete emotes with Tab:** type the start of an emote's name and press Tab. It's off by default.
- **Backup and restore:** save your settings, channels, favorites and chat words to a file, and put them back after a reinstall.

## 1.3.0 - 2026-11-07

- **7TV, BTTV and FFZ emotes:** they show in Twitch chat, including animated ones, with no other extension needed.
- **Emote names:** hover an emote to see what to type, and click it to add it to your message.
- **Chat font size:** it now also scales emotes and the badges next to names.

## 1.2.0 - 2026-11-06

- **Multi-stream:** drag a channel from the sidebar onto the page to watch two to four streams at once, from Twitch, YouTube and Kick together.
- **Favorites:** drag a channel to the top of the sidebar, or press its star, to keep channels from all three sites together in your own order.
- **Blocked and highlighted words:** remove chat messages containing the words you choose, or make them stand out, in Twitch, YouTube and Kick chat.
- **Hide For You:** a new setting removes the For You heading, the stories and the watch streak from the sidebar.
- **Kick volume:** the volume you pick carries over to the next stream and a browser restart.
- **Fixed:** YouTube Live and Kick Live no longer disappear from the collapsed sidebar after a reload.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-05

- **Kick Live:** the Kick channels you follow get their own section in the sidebar, with the same stream view and chat as YouTube streams.
- **Hide channels:** hide a YouTube or Kick channel you don't want to see, and show it again from the channel manager.
- **New settings:** hide what streamers' extensions show over the video, and turn off stream previews.
- **Sidebar highlight:** the Twitch channel you're watching stays highlighted.
- **Fixed:** long-running and 24/7 YouTube streams no longer go missing from your live subscriptions.
- **Fixed:** input boxes no longer take focus when the settings window opens.

## 1.0.1 - 2026-11-05

- **Stream previews:** hover a live channel in the sidebar to see an image of the stream.
- **Feedback:** the settings window links to report a bug, request a feature and open the GitHub page.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-05

- **YouTube live streams on Twitch:** watch them with chat in a Twitch-style view, from the sidebar or the Following page.
- **Floating chat, stacked mode and theatre mode:** for Twitch and YouTube streams, with Alt+C, Alt+S and Alt+T.
- **Stream quality and chat font size:** choose what streams start at, and how big chat's text is.
- **Cleaner chat and sidebar:** hide the parts of Twitch's chat and sidebar you don't use.
- **Auto-claim channel points:** off by default.

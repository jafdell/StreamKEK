Up to four streams at once.

Drag a channel from the sidebar onto the page to watch two to four streams, with their chats, from any mix of Twitch, YouTube and Kick. Drag between streams to resize them, drag a stream's name onto another to swap them, and choose which one has the sound.

Fullscreen, with chat floating over it.

Press Alt+C to watch Twitch, YouTube or Kick fullscreen with chat in a window you can move, resize and make see-through.

7TV, BTTV and FFZ emotes in Twitch chat.

Animated ones included, with no other extension. And in Twitch, YouTube and Kick chat alike, messages with the words you choose stand out or disappear, at one text size for all three.

And the smaller things.

The rest of what StreamKEK does. Click a name to read about it.

Sidebar

On the Following page too

Your live YouTube subscriptions and Kick follows also show on Twitch's Following page.

Add and hide channels

The + button adds YouTube and Kick channels you don't follow, and the eye on a channel hides it.

Favorites

Press a channel's star, or drag it to the top of the sidebar, to keep channels from all three sites together in your own order while they're live.

Go-live notifications off by default

Get a notification when a favorite starts a stream, also with no Twitch tab open.

Stream previews

Hover a live channel in the sidebar to see an image of its stream.

Cleaner sidebar

Hide For You, Live Channels, Recommended Categories and Viewers Also Watch, and turn off the YouTube Live and Kick Live sections.

Player

Stacked mode Alt+S

The stream at full width with chat under it, for narrow windows.

Theatre mode Alt+T

On YouTube and Kick streams, like Twitch's own.

Audio only Alt+A

Listen to a Twitch stream or video without the picture, which saves data and battery.

Volume boost

Make a quiet Twitch stream or video up to four times louder with the button beside the volume, until you reload or change channel.

Stream quality

Choose what streams start at on Twitch, YouTube and Kick, up to 1440p.

Keep quality in background tabs off by default

Stop Twitch lowering a live stream to 360p when its tab is in the background.

Reload player on errors

Twitch's player reloads itself when a stream stops with an error, instead of you doing it by hand.

Start when a channel goes live off by default

Wait on an offline Twitch channel and the stream starts by itself when it goes live, with no refresh.

About the stream and channel

Scroll down under a YouTube or Kick stream, like under a Twitch one, to see a YouTube stream's description, or a Kick channel's followers, description, links and panels.

Cleaner player

Hide what streamers' Twitch extensions show over the video.

Chat

Autocomplete emotes with Tab off by default

Type the start of an emote's name in Twitch chat and press Tab.

Latest emote in the chat box

The empty Twitch chat box shows the name of the emote chat used last, and Tab puts it in your message.

When you followed a channel

Type /followed in Twitch chat to see the date, shown only to you. Your own card in chat shows it too.

Cleaner chat

Hide Twitch chat's timestamps, top strip, drops, pinned messages and Hype Train.

Twitch's pages

Sort order that sticks

The sort you pick on Browse, live channels and category pages stays in new tabs and after a restart, instead of going back to Recommended For You.

Auto-claim channel points off by default

Only in a tab where the stream is playing.

Settings

One searchable window

Open it from StreamKEK's icon in Twitch's top bar or the browser toolbar.

Backup and restore

Save your settings, channels, favorites and chat words to a file, and put them back after a reinstall.

What's new

See what each version added, there or in the changelog. After an update, a small New tag marks it until you open it.

Privacy

StreamKEK has no server, no account and no analytics. Nothing you do with it is sent to its developer. What it saves stays in your browser, and your sign-ins to Twitch, YouTube and Kick only go to the site they belong to.

The privacy policy lists what it saves, what it sends and where, and what Twitch can see.

Code and feedback

StreamKEK's code is public on GitHub, under MIT with the Commons Clause: free to use, change and share, but not to sell. Pull requests are welcome. For a bigger change, open an issue first so we can talk it through.

Report a bug or request a feature. Each opens a short form on GitHub, which needs a GitHub account.

Also there: the changelog, the license and how the code is laid out.

Install

StreamKEK works in Chrome, Brave and other Chromium browsers, with no API key and no extra account. After installing it, reload any open Twitch tabs.

To play streams and let you chat inside Twitch, StreamKEK allows YouTube's and Kick's cookies on twitch.tv. This is why your browser warns about cookie settings when you install it.

Watch YouTube and Kick streams inside Twitch.

StreamKEK is a free browser extension. It brings YouTube and Kick streams and their chat into Twitch, lets you watch up to four streams side by side, and gives you more control over Twitch's player, chat and sidebar.

Add to Chrome

For Chrome, Brave and other Chromium browsers. No API key and no extra account.

Unofficial: not affiliated with or endorsed by Twitch, YouTube or Kick.

Watching

YouTube and Kick streams inside Twitch
Your live YouTube subscriptions and Kick follows show in Twitch's sidebar and on the Following page, and open with their chat in a Twitch-style view. Add other channels with the + button, and hide the ones you don't want.
Favorites
Press a channel's star, or drag it to the top of the sidebar, to keep channels from all three sites together in your own order while they're live.
Go-live notifications
Get a notification when a favorite starts a stream, also with no Twitch tab open. Off by default.
Stream previews
Hover a live channel in the sidebar to see an image of its stream.

Multi-stream

Drag a channel from the sidebar onto the page to watch two to four streams at once, with their chats, from any mix of Twitch, YouTube and Kick. Drag between streams to resize them, drag a stream's name onto another to swap them, and choose which one has the sound.

Player

Floating chat Alt+C
Watch Twitch, YouTube or Kick fullscreen with chat in a window you can move, resize and make see-through.
Stacked mode Alt+S
The stream at full width with chat under it, for narrow windows.
Theatre mode Alt+T
On YouTube and Kick streams, like Twitch's own.
Audio only Alt+A
Listen to a Twitch stream or video without the picture, which saves data and battery.
Stream quality
Choose what streams start at on Twitch, YouTube and Kick, up to 1440p.
Keep quality in background tabs
Stop Twitch lowering a live stream to 360p when its tab is in the background. Off by default.
Reload player on errors
Twitch's player reloads itself when a stream stops with an error, instead of you doing it by hand.
Start when a channel goes live
Wait on an offline Twitch channel and the stream starts by itself when it goes live, with no refresh. Off by default.

Chat

7TV, BTTV and FFZ emotes
See them in Twitch chat, animated ones included, with no other extension. Pick one from the button in the chat box, which lists your recently used emotes at the top, click one in chat to add it to your message, or type its name.
Autocomplete emotes with Tab
Type the start of an emote's name in Twitch chat and press Tab. Off by default.
Latest emote in the chat box
The empty Twitch chat box shows the name of the emote chat used last, and Tab puts it in your message.
Blocked and highlighted words
Remove messages containing the words you choose, or make them stand out, in Twitch, YouTube and Kick chat.
See deleted messages
Keep reading a message in Twitch chat after a moderator deletes it. Off by default.
When you followed a channel
Type /followed in Twitch chat to see the date, shown only to you. Your own card in chat shows it too.
Chat font size
One text size for Twitch, YouTube and Kick chat.
Cleaner chat
Hide Twitch chat's timestamps, top strip, drops, pinned messages and Hype Train.

A tidier Twitch

Sort order that sticks
The sort you pick on Browse, live channels and category pages stays in new tabs and after a restart, instead of going back to Recommended For You.
Cleaner sidebar
Hide For You, Live Channels, Recommended Categories and Viewers Also Watch, and turn off the YouTube Live and Kick Live sections.
Cleaner player
Hide what streamers' Twitch extensions show over the video.
Auto-claim channel points
Only in a tab where the stream is playing. Off by default.

Settings

One searchable window
Open it from StreamKEK's icon in Twitch's top bar or the browser toolbar.
Backup and restore
Save your settings, channels, favorites and chat words to a file, and put them back after a reinstall.

Themes

16 themes recolor Twitch, the settings window and the YouTube and Kick stream view, besides Twitch's own dark and light. The seasons, Christmas and Halloween also move a little, like snow falling behind the sidebar, which a switch turns off. Kick's chat stays dark in a light theme.

Pick one below. This page takes the theme the way Twitch does: its colors, its backdrop where the page is empty and, in the Seasons and Specials, its motion. The drawings change with it.

Privacy

StreamKEK has no server, no account and no analytics. Nothing you do with it is sent to its developer. What it saves stays in your browser, and your sign-ins to Twitch, YouTube and Kick only go to the site they belong to.

The privacy policy lists what it saves, what it sends and where, and what Twitch can see.

Install

StreamKEK works in Chrome, Brave and other Chromium browsers, with no API key and no extra account. After installing it, reload any open Twitch tabs.

To play streams and let you chat inside Twitch, StreamKEK allows YouTube's and Kick's cookies on twitch.tv. This is why your browser warns about cookie settings when you install it.
